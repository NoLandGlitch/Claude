Our Story

From a Single Office to California's Most Trusted Realty

Gateway Realty was born in 2009 when founder Michael Gateway — a 20-year veteran of the California real estate market — saw a gap between what clients needed and what the industry delivered: real expertise, honest advice, and agents who truly listened.

Starting with just four agents and a rented office, we grew by doing one thing consistently: putting our clients first. Word spread. Referrals came. Today, our 42-agent team spans the greater California region, but our commitment to the personal touch that built us remains unchanged.

"Real estate is about people. The moment you forget that, you've lost the whole point."

— Michael Gateway, Founder & CEO

We believe in full transparency, local expertise, and relationships that last well beyond closing day. That's the Gateway difference.

Our Core Values

Every decision we make comes back to these six principles.

🤝
Integrity First

We say what we mean and mean what we say. Our clients receive honest assessments — even when the truth is hard to hear — because trust is everything.

🏆
Local Expertise

Our agents live and work in the communities they serve. That's not a marketing line — it's the reason we consistently outperform out-of-market competitors.

⚡
Results-Driven

We're measured by one thing: outcomes for our clients. Faster closings, better prices, fewer surprises. That's the standard we hold ourselves to every day.

🌱
Community Focus

We invest back into the neighborhoods we work in — from local sponsorships to charity auctions. When the community thrives, so does everyone in it.

💡
Innovation

We embrace modern tools — virtual tours, market analytics, real-time data — to give our clients an edge in any market condition.

Transparency

No hidden fees, no vague answers. Our clients always know where their transaction stands, what it costs, and why we're recommending each step.
